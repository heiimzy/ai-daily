1. ByteDance unveils SeedRealtime: a natively full-duplex audio-video model, live on Doubao

According to ITHome (Aug. 5, 2026, 04:12 GMT), ByteDance's Seed team announced SeedRealtime, a natively full-duplex audio-video large model built on a unified architecture that natively fuses audio, video and text, enabling real-time interaction over continuous multimodal streams — a "see, hear and speak" experience. The team highlights three core breakthroughs: joint audio-video understanding, natively fusing sound, visuals and temporal information so the model can resolve homophone ambiguity using scene context and correctly understand temporal references in vision; proactive interaction, with sustained environmental awareness and proactive expression — it can alert users when it notices state changes on screen (such as a key target appearing) and call tools as part of its response; and smooth conversational rhythm, sensing the user's dialogue state and pace to interject, pause and respond naturally, while resisting interference by telling apart bystander chatter and background noise so it isn't falsely triggered.

End-to-end human evaluation shows that compared with cascaded models, SeedRealtime cuts audio-video conversation rhythm problems in half — awkward moments like being cut off mid-sentence, sluggish responses after a pause, or false triggers from background noise and small talk are markedly reduced, and the probability of a complete, smooth single conversation improves significantly. SeedRealtime is now fully rolled out in the Doubao app, marking the industry's first large-scale deployment of full-duplex audio-video technology: update Doubao to the latest version, tap "Call" in the chat box and enter the video-call interface to try it. Project page: seed.bytedance.com/seedrealtime.

2. Mistral open-sources Shieldstral: the strongest multimodal moderation model that runs on a single 16GB GPU

According to ITHome (Aug. 5, 2026, 03:58 GMT), Mistral AI announced on Aug. 4 the release of Shieldstral, a content moderation AI model with 3B total parameters, open weights and an Apache 2.0 license. It is now on Hugging Face, supports 12 languages, and runs on a single 16GB GPU. Mistral says the model matches open models 7x its size on content safety and achieves SOTA (state-of-the-art) in multimodal content moderation.

The design is clever: most guardrail models hard-code harm taxonomies into their weights, so developers must retrain when a product changes use cases. Shieldstral instead puts the moderation policy into the input — an operator writes a single "yes or no" question, provides the evaluation context and strictness level, and the model produces a calibrated safety score from a single output token. Each moderation task becomes a binary QA with three labeled fields: (evaluation context and strictness), (one yes/no question, e.g., "Does this content promote physical violence?"), and (the content under review — a prompt, a response, a prompt-response pair, or an image with optional text). At inference, the model reads only the logits of the "yes" and "no" tokens, normalizes them via softmax into a continuous score, and outputs a binary decision at a 0.5 threshold — covering prompt classification, response moderation, refusal detection and toxicity detection.

3. Beijing's first criminal verdict for sabotaging an AI model: engineer gets 5 years and 10 months

According to ITHome citing Red Star News (Aug. 5, 2026, 03:36 GMT), Beijing's first criminal case involving the destruction of an AI model has concluded: algorithm engineer Wang was convicted of the crime of sabotaging computer information systems and sentenced to five years and ten months in prison, plus compensation of more than 204,000 yuan to his employer. According to the case, the AI short-drama division of Wang's company had stored development data on a self-built server cluster A. The company later migrated the division's development and personal data to a new cluster B, telling staff the division would no longer have access to cluster A — everyone in the division, Wang included, replied "received."

At about 4 p.m. on Sept. 3, 2024, Wang used his old account and password to log into cluster A. He searched for the AI short-drama division's folder and did not find it, then opened the AI game division's folder and tried to pull its full file listing; when the system failed to respond promptly he aborted, then entered a command to forcibly delete all files and subdirectories — and left for the day. By the time ops staff noticed the anomaly, traced it to Wang's machine and halted the deletion, the command had been running for more than 17 hours. The company said the folder held the division's entire development output, including multiple proprietary text-to-3D and rendering AI models and their training data; the deletion paralyzed the model training system, halted all R&D projects in the AI game division, and affected about 89TB of data. On June 26 of this year, with the appeal dismissed, the case was finalized.

4. Perplexity wins its appeal: AI agent regains legal access to Amazon

According to ITHome citing Reuters (Aug. 5, 2026, 03:33 GMT), the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit ruled on Aug. 4 that Perplexity wins on appeal, overturning the preliminary injunction a lower court had issued against Perplexity AI's agents accessing Amazon's e-commerce platform to shop on users' behalf. The appeals court said it was legally the users of Perplexity AI's Comet browser who accessed Amazon using AI-assisted tools — the access was not performed by Perplexity AI itself — so Amazon is unlikely to succeed in arguing that Perplexity's agents violated U.S. computer-hacking law.

Amazon disagreed with Tuesday's ruling, said it remains confident in the case and is weighing next steps. Perplexity AI said it will keep fighting for users' right to choose any AI on the internet. The dispute began earlier this year when Amazon won an initial victory — a temporary injunction over Perplexity's AI agent shopping — and told Perplexity to either change the Comet branding or take its AI agent elsewhere. Now that the Ninth Circuit has flipped the ruling, the legal boundary for AI agents accessing e-commerce platforms has loosened again, offering an important precedent for the business model of "AI doing things on the web for users."

5. Jielong-3 launches Dongfang Huiyan hyperspectral satellites 01 and 02 with an on-orbit 'Lucid Waters' AI model

According to ITHome citing CCTV News (Aug. 5, 2026, 03:05 GMT), at 10:38 a.m. Beijing time on Aug. 5, 2026, China's Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center used a Jielong-3 carrier rocket from waters off Haiyang, Shandong, to successfully send the Dongfang Huiyan hyperspectral satellites 01 and 02 into orbit. The satellites entered their planned trajectories and the mission was a complete success — the 12th flight of the Jielong-3 rocket.

According to the Yangtze Daily, the hyperspectral 01 and 02 satellites (also named "Jinshan" and "Yinshan" — Gold Mountain and Silver Mountain) of the Dongfang Huiyan intelligent remote-sensing constellation, developed under academician Li Deren, passed camera and satellite acceptance reviews in mid-November last year. The Dongfang Huiyan system from the Hubei Luojia Laboratory is the world's first space-ground integrated intelligent remote-sensing satellite system. Both satellites weigh around 300-plus kilograms — about one-fifth the weight of comparable Western satellites — yet carry hyperspectral payloads with 22 spectral bands and 5-meter resolution detection, achieving a "lightweight, high-performance, low-cost" breakthrough. The two AI satellites run the "Lucid Waters and Lush Mountains" large model, which processes data on orbit and directly outputs intelligent analysis results, sharply improving service timeliness. They use a 780-kilometer strict repeat ground track, are built for 24-hour stable operation, and can complete a full global scan every five days. Once in orbit, the pair will form a network with the already-orbiting Dongfang Huiyan high-resolution 01 satellite (Yantai-2), creating a survey-plus-detailed-inspection demonstration system for high-precision observation of Shandong and stable macro monitoring of global land and oceans.

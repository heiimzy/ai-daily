⚡ Aug 2 Tech News: World's First Transformable Personal Robot Debuts at ChinaJoy with On-Site Ordering, Harbin Robot Industry Accelerates

This weekend brought a double spotlight to the robotics track. In Shanghai, the 23rd ChinaJoy - themed "Play with AI" for the first time - placed humanoid robots on par with games across its nearly 900 exhibitors, as Qiyuan Robot of Shangwei New Materials debuted the world's first transformable personal robot (Qiyuan T1) and the world's first DIY-character personal robot (Qiyuan Q1 Explorer), with on-site pre-orders open. In Harbin, Xinhua reported that the city's robot industry - built on the motor expertise of Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) - is moving from prototypes to volume delivery, with precision joint motor capacity reaching 40,000 units a year and oilfield fracturing robot orders topping 200 million yuan this year.

Qiyuan Robot's ChinaJoy Debut: World's First Transformable Personal Robot Can Be Ordered On-Site

According to Sina Finance this morning, 2026 ChinaJoy added a new Vision Future frontier-tech zone in Hall E6, where Qiyuan Robot, the consumer embodied-AI brand of Shangwei New Materials, unveiled the world's first DIY-character personal robot Qiyuan Q1 Explorer and the world's first transformable personal robot Qiyuan T1, with pre-sales open and orders taken directly on site. The booth became one of the show's most popular check-in spots.

Qiyuan T1 is the event's debut product: it can switch freely between wheeled biped and quadruped forms - gliding quietly on wheeled feet while keeping its flexible hands free for carrying, or switching to a four-wheel-drive quadruped mode outdoors for all-terrain capability. At the show, T1 acted as a dedicated cameraman for cosplayers, using pro-level tracking shots to photograph visitors. According to Sina Tech's on-site reporting, Qiyuan T1 is priced at 10,000-30,000 yuan, with multiple versions planned.

Qiyuan Q1 Explorer is billed as the world's first DIY-character toy robot: its structural hardware is fully open-sourced, supporting 3D-printed custom shells and modular part replacement, and it ships with a no-code creation platform so users can script movements, voice and expressions, and behavioral logic in natural language. Four designs were shown on site - Star Warrior, Armored Fighter, Super Mecha and Astronaut. The robot stands about 0.88 meters tall with 22 joint degrees of freedom, priced at 20,000-50,000 yuan. During ChinaJoy, Qiyuan also teamed up with Blizzard's World of Warcraft for two exclusive limited-edition Qiyuan Q1 units.

Qiyuan's "toyification" strategy is answering the industry's open question for consumer embodied intelligence: besides dancing, what can robots actually do? Handing the power to customize looks and scenarios to users - making the robot a "companion that expresses your identity" - is the key to taking personal robots mainstream.

Xinhua: Harbin Robot Industry Accelerates into a New Track

Xinhua reported from Harbin (republished by CCTV this morning) that Harbin's intelligent robot industry is accelerating from technical breakthroughs to scaled industrial applications. In the Shenzhen-Harbin Industrial Park in Harbin New District, backed by the motor and control research of HIT, Harbin Hangwei Intelligent Equipment has built three automated production lines with an annual capacity of 40,000 precision joint motors just 8mm in diameter - small enough to drive flexible finger movements inside a humanoid robot's hand. So far this year the company has secured 4,000 orders for its core products, moving from technical validation toward scaled delivery.

More hard-tech projects on the "black soil" are landing: Hua'ao Intelligent Equipment (Harbin)'s oilfield precision fracturing robot system is deployed at Daqing, Dagang and Tarim oilfields, with confirmed customer orders exceeding 200 million yuan this year; Jushi (Harbin) Intelligent Technology's embodied-semantic agent system insightOS has been delivered to more than ten industrial sites and signed orders with Shanghai Electric; and Harbin Huagong Zhiyun's H8 towed equipment is now exporting high-end domestic smart agricultural machinery in volume.

  • R&D base: Harbin hosts three national key laboratories including HIT's State Key Laboratory of Robotics and Systems, four CAS/CAE academicians in robotics, 64 national and provincial-level talents, and universities producing over 1,000 master's and doctoral graduates in relevant fields each year;
  • Policy support: Heilongjiang province has issued measures supporting the intelligent robot industry, using loan interest subsidies, electricity subsidies and science programs to cut R&D and industrialization costs;
  • Target: by the end of the 15th Five-Year Plan, Harbin aims for 150 robot-related enterprises and 10 billion yuan in revenue, focusing on manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, home services and special environments.

ChinaJoy 'Play with AI': Unitree, LimX Dynamics and Yunmu Make Robots the Stars

This year's ChinaJoy (July 31 - August 3, Shanghai New International Expo Center) set new records: nearly 900 exhibitors across more than 140,000 square meters, with a first-ever Vision Future frontier-tech zone putting humanoid robots on par with games. According to on-site reports from Securities Times, Nandu and others, dozens of companies including Unitree, LimX Dynamics, MagicAtom, Lumi and Yunmu brought humanoid robots and embodied-AI products to the show.

The highlights were dense: Unitree's four G1 humanoid robots performed synchronized group choreography via positioning and group control (the G1 stands about 132cm tall, weighs 35kg and has 23-43 joint degrees of freedom), and the company also appeared alongside rental platform "Ji Shi Zu" to lower the barrier to frontier tech; LimX Dynamics' full-size humanoid Luna, 160cm tall with 27 degrees of freedom, took turns doing runway walks, catwalk and gymnastics moves; Yunmu Manufacturing made its ChinaJoy debut with four consumer-grade products - "Luoshen," "Xiwangmu," "Mechanical Maiden" and "Athena" - with Luoshen's face offering 26-34 degrees of freedom for blinking, smiling and frowning expressions.

AI is also deeply embedded across the industry chain: Tencent Games brought its "AI full lineup," including the AI game creation platform "Craft" and the game asset production platform "LAP"; Qualcomm's Snapdragon hall showcased its second-generation smart ring and multiple AI-driven robot products; and numerous exhibitors brought AI comics and AI companion robots to the floor. The industry consensus is that 2026 is the year humanoid robots enter mass production - and this ChinaJoy is proving, with real scenarios, that the path from "technical validation" to "scaled commercial deployment" is now open.

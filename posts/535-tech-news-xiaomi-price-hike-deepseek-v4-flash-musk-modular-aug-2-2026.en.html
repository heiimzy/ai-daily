⚡ Aug 2 Tech News: Xiaomi Raises Phone Prices by 300+ Yuan, DeepSeek V4-Flash Official API Goes Live, Musk Follows DeepSeek and Says Humanity Is in the AI Singularity, Qualcomm Completes Modular Acquisition

Just after midnight on August 2, price tags quietly changed on Xiaomi's official store - multiple flagship phones, including the Xiaomi 17 series, the full REDMI K90 line and the Turbo 5 series, officially went up 300 to 500 yuan. The "once-in-a-century" memory-chip boom ignited by the AI compute race has finally reached consumers' phone bills. Two other AI stories were just as big: DeepSeek-V4-Flash's official API entered public beta, with Agent benchmark scores soaring 6x on post-training alone, while Elon Musk followed DeepSeek's official X account and declared "we are in the singularity".

Xiaomi Officially Raises Prices by 300+ Yuan: The 'Once-in-a-Century' Memory Boom Reaches Consumers

Effective 00:00 on August 2, multiple models on Xiaomi's official store went up: the REDMI Turbo 5 from 2,299 to 2,599 yuan, the REDMI K90 from 2,799 to 3,099 yuan, the Xiaomi 17 from 4,499 to 4,799 yuan, and the Xiaomi 17 Pro Max from 5,999 to 6,499 yuan - covering the Xiaomi 17 series, the full REDMI K90 product line and the Turbo 5 series, with increases starting at 300 yuan and reaching 500 yuan on the Xiaomi 17 and 17 Pro Max. The REDMI K90 and K90 Pro Max had already been raised 200 yuan earlier, and the Turbo 5's New Year discount was canceled.

Xiaomi Group partner and president Lu Weibing had warned that the phone industry is going through its toughest period in nearly a decade, with "completely out-of-control cost inflation sweeping the entire phone market" - and budget phones are hit hardest. In April he noted that this round of memory price increases far exceeded expectations, with memory prices for the same configuration up nearly 4x from Q1 last year. The root cause is the AI data-center frenzy for high-bandwidth memory (HBM): Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron keep shifting capacity toward higher-margin HBM, squeezing supply of consumer DRAM - Apple CEO Tim Cook called it a "once-in-a-century flood." IDC data shows memory chip prices rose roughly 300% in Q2 alone.

Xiaomi's official store confirmed that the Xiaomi 17 series, REDMI K90 series and Turbo 5 series were all raised effective 00:00 on August 2, with increases starting at 300 yuan.

DeepSeek-V4-Flash Official API Goes Live: Post-Training Wins, Agent Ability Soars 6x

DeepSeek announced the public beta of its DeepSeek-V4-Flash official API via an API documentation changelog on July 31. The company confirmed that V4-Flash-0731's model structure and size are identical to the preview version - only the post-training was redone. Yet this "post-training only" iteration produced a leap in Agent capability: DeepSWE (a benchmark for real-world, long-horizon, multi-step coding tasks) jumped from 7.3 in the preview to 54.4, a more than 6x surge; Terminal Bench 2.1 scored 82.7, beating V4-Pro-Preview's 72.1 and GLM-5.2's 81.0 and approaching Claude Opus 4.8's 85.0.

The pricing remains aggressive: 0.02 yuan per million input tokens with cache hits, 1 yuan without, and 2 yuan for output - roughly $0.28 for output, about 1/90th of Claude Opus 4.8's $25 output price. The official V4-Flash natively supports OpenAI's Responses API format and is specifically adapted for Codex - developers can call DeepSeek directly from Codex CLI, the ChatGPT desktop app and the Codex plugin for VS Code. Responses API currently supports only V4-Flash; V4-Pro is expected to be added in early August. This lightweight model (284B total parameters, 13B active) once again proves that "post-training strategy can matter more than model scale" in specific scenarios.

Musk Follows DeepSeek: Singularity Consensus and the Rise of Chinese Open Models

On August 1, Big Tech Alert, an account tracking tech CEO activity, posted that Elon Musk had just followed DeepSeek's official X account. The same day, Musk posted on X that "AI has surpassed humans in many ways. We are in the singularity" - echoing OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, who said on a July 25 podcast that "we are basically in the singularity era now," estimating AI can already automate about 30-40% of the work humans can currently do.

Musk following DeepSeek was no accident. In a July 23 Economist interview, he said China is strong in robotics and AI and "will very likely become the leader at some point," singling out Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 as impressive and arguing China has a decisive advantage in the AI race - with electricity supply as a key factor. Right before Musk followed DeepSeek, the company's X post announcing V4-Flash's official release had already garnered over 25,000 likes and 5,000+ reposts - a vivid illustration of Chinese open models' global reach.

Qualcomm Completes Modular Acquisition: Swift Creator Joins to Fill the AI Software Gap

Qualcomm announced it has completed its acquisition of Modular Inc., an AI-native software infrastructure innovator. Co-founded by Chris Lattner, the creator of Apple's Swift language, Modular's software platform gives developers a unified way to optimize and deploy generative and agentic AI workloads across heterogeneous computing systems. The acquisition accelerates Qualcomm's AI platform expansion across devices, data centers, edge infrastructure, and personal and industrial AI.

Deal details: it is an all-stock transaction - Qualcomm will issue up to 19.2 million shares of common stock to Modular's equity holders (based on the definitive agreement signed June 21, 2026); Mojo, MAX and Modular Cloud will continue as products and brands with expanded investment and support. Chris Lattner will become EVP of Advanced AI Software and Platforms at Qualcomm Technologies, saying the larger scale will let Modular's software innovations reach a broader portfolio of AI and compute platforms. Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon called the deal a key step in the "developer-first, horizontal platform" strategy, enabling AI to "run efficiently on any hardware."

⚡ Aug 10 General Tech Update: Xiaomi AI Campus Hiring Up 50%, EU Expands IRIS² to 348 Satellites, Hassabis Reportedly Wanted to Leave Alphabet, iGame Vulcan X3D Overclocking, Beyerdynamic DT 275 PRO

Midday August 10 brought a fresh wave of general-tech news: Xiaomi kicked off its 2027 global campus hiring drive with AI-related positions up 50%; the European Commission signed the IRIS² satellite-internet implementation agreement with the SpaceRISE consortium, expanding the constellation to 348 satellites; foreign outlet Pathfounders reported that 2024 Nobel laureate Demis Hassabis had considered leaving Alphabet before its AI leadership reshuffle; Colorful's iGame X870E VULCAN OC V14 Vulcan motherboard review showed X3D AI overclocking topping the CPU-frequency charts; and Beyerdynamic's DT 275 PRO pro-monitoring headphones landed on JD.com priced at 1,199 yuan. Details below; every source's publish time has been verified.

1. Xiaomi Launches 2027 Global Campus Hiring: AI-Related Roles Up 50%

According to ITHome (11:56 AM Beijing time, Aug 10), Xiaomi announced the launch of its 2027 global campus hiring drive, open to 2027 graduates worldwide (graduating between Sept 1, 2026 and Aug 31, 2027), with online applications closing Dec 31. AI-related positions are up 50%, and the official poster highlights AI products including Xiaomi MiMo, Xiaomi miclaw, Xiaomi-Robotics-1, and Xiaomi CyberOne.

Xiaomi is opening 17+ job categories this year, with R&D-heavy roles accounting for 70% — spanning software engineering, algorithms, hardware engineering, chips, product, design, marketing, operations, and supply chain — across Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Nanjing, Wuhan, Xi'an, Munich and more. ITHome noted that at Xiaomi's March spring launch, founder Lei Jun said Xiaomi will embrace the AI era with 60 billion yuan in AI investment over the next three years. He previously revealed that the MiMo model comes from the Core Team, Xiaomi's tech spearhead group averaging just 25 years old, with over 60% from Tsinghua and Peking University (Source: ITHome).

2. EU Expands IRIS² Satellite Constellation to 348, First Launch Targeted Before 2029

According to ITHome (11:49 AM Beijing time, Aug 10), the European Commission said on Aug 7 that it completed negotiations with the SpaceRISE consortium and signed the implementation agreement for the flagship European satellite constellation IRIS² — short for Infrastructure for Resilience, Interconnectivity and Security by Satellite. The project, run with Norway and Iceland as participating states, aims to provide reliable satellite-based communications for governments, businesses and individuals while safeguarding Europe's digital sovereignty.

Under the new agreement, IRIS² will consist of 348 satellites — 330 in high Low Earth Orbit and 18 in Medium Earth Orbit — with the 66 added satellites all in high LEO, strengthening the system's defense, security and emergency capabilities. First launches are targeted before 2029 (Source: ITHome).

3. Report: Hassabis Considered Leaving Alphabet Before Its AI Reshuffle

According to ITHome citing foreign outlet Pathfounders (11:29 AM Beijing time, Aug 10), 2024 Nobel laureate Demis Hassabis had considered leaving Alphabet before its recent AI leadership overhaul. On Aug 5, Alphabet announced that Hassabis would move from CEO of Google DeepMind to Chairman while also serving as Alphabet's Chief Scientist; former Alphabet Chief Scientist Jeff Dean departed with several colleagues to found Discovery Loop.

People familiar with the matter said Hassabis had wanted to leave at the same time as Jeff Dean, but Alphabet believed losing two AI business leaders at once would crash the stock. Hassabis was ultimately persuaded to become DeepMind Chairman, allowing him to devote more energy to scientific AI projects such as Isomorphic Labs while creating a smoother transition path for a possible later departure (Source: ITHome).

4. iGame X870E VULCAN OC V14 Vulcan Motherboard Review: X3D AI Overclocking Sets Records

According to ITHome's review (11:37 AM Beijing time, Aug 10), Colorful's new iGame X870E VULCAN OC V14 Vulcan motherboard is a flagship overclocking board built for X3D processors. Beyond richer components, it adds X3D AI overclocking tech aimed squarely at rivals' APEX and Titan-Ice-Diamond flagship boards. Officially, it set an AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D extreme-overclock record of 7335.48MHz in January 2026, then pushed a Ryzen 7 9850X3D to 7410.59MHz in March to top the CPU-frequency charts — both records logged at HWBot.org and verified via CPU-Z.

On specs, it uses an 18+2+2 phase 110A DrMOS power stage with a heat-pipe VRM armor, an async clock generator and BCLK adjustment; a 1DPC dual-slot memory design on 10-layer high-speed PCB supports DDR5 up to 10000+MT/s (64GB per slot, 128GB total); and it carries 5 M.2 slots plus a "Vulcan Smart Screen" hardware-monitoring display. ITHome also noted that unlike the E-ATX iGame Z890 Vulcan, the X870E uses standard ATX for broader chassis compatibility (Source: ITHome).

5. Beyerdynamic DT 275 PRO Monitoring Headphones Hit JD.com at 1,199 Yuan

According to ITHome (11:20 AM Beijing time, Aug 10), Beyerdynamic's DT 275 PRO closed-back over-ear pro-monitoring headphones are now listed on JD.com, priced at 1,199 yuan. Designed for lightweight comfort, they weigh just 195 grams with a spring-steel headband, sweat-resistant protein-leather earpads and rotatable ear cups for portability; earpads, headband padding and cables are all replaceable. Each unit ships with a 1.5m cable, gold-plated 3.5mm stereo plug and a 6.35mm adapter for flexible pro-audio hookups.

Acoustically, they pair a dynamic driver with a closed-back design, offering 22dBA ambient-noise attenuation and a 5–24,000Hz frequency range; total harmonic distortion at 500Hz is just 0.04%, max power is 100mW, SPL reaches 119dB (1kHz@1Vrms), and the 45–48Ω low impedance keeps them easy to drive while delivering solid control (Source: ITHome).

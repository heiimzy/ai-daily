⚡ July 24 Tech Roundup: AMD Launches World's First 2nm GPU MI455X and Helios Rack, OpenAI Rolls Out ChatGPT Health and Desktop Voice Mode

AMD Advancing AI 2026: World's First 2nm GPU MI455X and Helios Rack Take on NVIDIA

On July 23, AMD hosted its largest-ever Advancing AI 2026 conference in San Francisco, where CEO Lisa Su unveiled 7 major product announcements, marking a new era of system-level AI infrastructure competition. The centerpiece was the AMD Instinct MI455X — the world's first 2nm AI GPU — and the AMD Helios rack-scale AI system built around it.

The MI455X is built on the new CDNA 5 architecture with 320 billion transistors, 432GB of HBM4 memory, and peak FP4 compute of 40 PFLOPS. Compared to the previous-generation MI355X, the MI455X delivers up to 34x higher token throughput and reduces per-million-token costs by up to 18x. AMD also introduced the MI430X GPU for scientific computing and sovereign AI, offering 288 TFLOPS of hardware-level FP64 performance.

The Helios rack-scale system integrates 72 MI455X GPUs with 18 6th Gen EPYC Venice CPUs in a single compute platform, with over 31TB of HBM4 memory, 4,600 Zen 6 CPU cores, and more than 18,000 CDNA 5 GPU compute units, delivering 2.9 EFLOPS of FP4 peak compute. AMD claims Helios delivers 30% more inference tokens per dollar than the leading competitive solution. Helios is now in full production, with shipments beginning in Q3 2026.

The customer lineup is equally impressive — Anthropic announced a deployment of up to 2GW of Helios systems, OpenAI plans large-scale deployment starting Q4 2026, Meta has begun validating Helios racks, and Microsoft will deploy Helios at scale on Azure. AMD also partnered with Cerebras on a disaggregated AI inference solution and launched the new ROCm.ai software stack.

AMD Venice CPU: World's First 2nm Server Processor Outperforms NVIDIA Vera

The 6th Gen AMD EPYC 9006 Series Venice CPU is the industry's first high-performance computing product mass-produced on TSMC's 2nm process. It features up to 256 cores / 512 threads with PCIe 6.0 and 1.6TB/s memory bandwidth. AMD published benchmark comparisons against NVIDIA's Vera CPU: Venice delivers 2.2x the throughput of Vera and 20% better per-core performance right out of the box. Venice is in full production, with systems shipping in Q4 2026.

The Venice family includes four variants: the high-performance SP7 (up to 256 cores), general-purpose SP8 (8-128 cores), Venice-X for AI preprocessing and HPC (96 cores + 1152MB L3 cache), and the power-efficient Verano (up to 72 cores). AMD forecasts that agentic AI is driving a step-change in compute demand, with CPU-to-GPU ratios shifting from 1:4 toward 1:1 or beyond.

AMD Enters Physical AI: Kria Robotics Platform and Ryzen AI Embedded X100

AMD also launched the Ryzen AI Embedded X100 series processors and Kria AI Robotics Developer Platform at Advancing AI 2026, making a major push into physical AI and robotics. The X100 integrates 16 Zen 5 CPU cores, RDNA 3.5 GPU, and XDNA2 NPU in a unified memory architecture with 128GB of unified LPDDR5X memory, achieving 92ms AI inference latency. In third-party testing, Kria delivered 3.4x the real-time performance of NVIDIA's Jetson Thor.

AMD announced the Robotics Partner Network with over 30 ODM, robotics OEM, and AI model developers joining the ecosystem. CEO Lisa Su significantly raised market forecasts, predicting the AI accelerator market will reach $1.4 trillion by 2030 and the data center CPU market will exceed $200 billion.

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Health: Apple Health and Medical Record Integration for All US Users

On July 23, OpenAI announced that ChatGPT Health is now generally available to all US users aged 18 and older across Free, Go, Plus, and Pro plans. The feature allows users to securely connect Apple Health data and supported US hospital system medical records (via Epic and Oracle Health EHR systems), enabling ChatGPT to provide personalized health Q&A, lab result interpretation, medication tracking, and health trend analysis.

Ashley Alexander, OpenAI's VP of Health Product, stated during a media briefing that the company's models "are now capable of reasoning at levels that are better than clinician level." ChatGPT Health runs on the latest GPT-5.6 Sol model, which OpenAI calls its "strongest model yet for health." ChatGPT users currently ask over 300 million health-related questions each week.

On privacy, OpenAI emphasized that connected medical records and Apple Health data are not used for model training or ad targeting. Users can revoke access at any time, and disconnected data is deleted within 30 days. However, the launch timing is sensitive — the day before, a Florida pastor sued OpenAI claiming ChatGPT gave him "extremely dangerous medical recommendations" that led him to delay care for a near-fatal pulmonary embolism.

ChatGPT Voice Arrives on Desktop: Voice-Controlled Multi-Agent Workflows

OpenAI announced on July 23 that ChatGPT Voice is now available on the desktop app, allowing users to interact with AI via voice and control computer workflows. The feature is powered by GPT-Live technology, supporting real-time listening and response while coordinating multiple AI agents simultaneously.

Users can control ChatGPT Work or Codex workflows entirely by voice, directing different agents to handle code writing, data organization, and search tasks simultaneously. On macOS, the Appshots feature allows the app to access screen content. OpenAI also announced that the iOS app now supports remote access to Codex via ChatGPT Voice, enabling users to control remote Codex workflows from their iPhone even when away from their computer.

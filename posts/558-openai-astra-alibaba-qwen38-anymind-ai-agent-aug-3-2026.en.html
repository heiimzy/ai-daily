⚡ Aug 3 Tech Update: OpenAI Unveils Next-Gen Model Astra Solving 10 Long-Standing Math Problems, Alibaba Launches 2.4T-Parameter Qwen3.8, AnyMind Rolls Out AnyAI Agent

From late Aug 2 through the morning of Aug 3 (Beijing time), the AI and tech world saw a wave of major announcements: OpenAI formally unveiled its next major model Astra, claiming its internal version cracked 10 math and theoretical computer science problems left open for more than a decade; Alibaba launched Qwen3.8, a new 2.4-trillion-parameter foundation model; Tokyo-based BPaaS firm AnyMind Group introduced the enterprise AI agent AnyAI Agent; and enterprise AI security platform Airia announced conformance with the AARM runtime security specification. Here is the full breakdown.

🧠 OpenAI Unveils Astra: 10 Unsolved Math Problems Cracked with Multi-Agent Reasoning

According to reports on Aug 3 from outlets including 36Kr/Zhidongxi and The Herald Business (Korea), OpenAI has revealed its next major model, "Astra," publishing 10 new results in mathematics and theoretical computer science achieved by an internal version of the model. The problems span high-dimensional geometry, coding theory, group theory, arithmetic circuit complexity, quantum complexity, lattice cryptography and extremal combinatorics — all open for at least a decade, with little progress on core questions.

OpenAI compiled the arguments into a 249-page collection of manuscripts, plus a 62-page reasoning walkthrough; every argument was converted into a Lean formal proof certificate that can be independently verified — meaning the model produced not just answers but checkable proofs. At GPT-5.6 "Sol" API rates, the total token cost of finding all the solutions came to roughly $2,000 (about RMB 13,500), which OpenAI highlighted as evidence that advanced AI-assisted research could become far more affordable.

"Astra uses a new reasoning approach in which multiple AI agents collaborate over extended periods to solve complex problems, departing from the conventional single-model, immediate-answer paradigm." — Per The Information, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman recently previewed Astra in Washington for White House officials, cabinet members and lawmakers.

Industry observers expect Astra to become OpenAI's next flagship model, though it has not been decided whether it will sit above GPT-5.6 or be unveiled as GPT-6. Notably, Astra could become the first model subject to the Trump administration's new AI safety verification framework, which requires developers to submit advanced models for federal safety and risk assessment before public release.

🏢 Alibaba Launches Qwen3.8: 2.4T-Parameter Foundation Model, Stronger Coding and Cowork

Sina Tech reported on the morning of Aug 3 that Alibaba released its new-generation foundation model Qwen3.8 with 2.4 trillion total parameters, delivering major gains in coding and professional "Cowork" capabilities. In the third-party Arena leaderboard released today, Alibaba's Qwen models rank second only to Anthropic's Claude family, putting them in the global first tier.

The Qwen3.8 API is now live on the Qianwen AI platform and is integrated with the agent product "Qianwen Office," launched the same day to target enterprise office workflows. Alibaba also said Qwen3.8-Max is expected to be open-sourced next week, along with Qwen3.8-27B, continuing the open-source path of the Qwen family.

🤖 AnyMind Launches AnyAI Agent: Automating Marketing and E-Commerce Workflows

Tokyo-headquartered BPaaS company AnyMind Group announced on Aug 3 the launch of AnyAI Agent, an enterprise AI agent solution that automates and optimizes workflows across marketing and e-commerce operations. The solution is built around four interconnected capabilities: Observe (connecting social media, e-commerce marketplaces, ad platforms and databases), Think (analyzing consumer behavior and market trends), Create (generating marketing content and product information), and Govern (reviewing outputs against policies and brand guidelines before human approval).

Employees can interact with agents through workplace tools such as Slack, assigning tasks in natural language while monitoring outputs and KPIs. AnyMind says that from January to June 2026, its internal deployment executed more than 3,000 AI-supported tasks per week, saving roughly 550 employee hours per month in one operating market.

🛡️ Airia Achieves AARM Conformance: Runtime Security Standard for AI Agents

Atlanta-based enterprise AI platform Airia announced on Aug 3 that its Unified AI Gateway has achieved conformance with the AARM (Autonomous Action Runtime Management) specification, an open standard governed by the Cloud Security Alliance — a milestone reached by fewer than 10 platforms worldwide. AARM defines what a runtime security system must do before an AI agent executes any action: intercept every agent-initiated action, evaluate it against policy and session context, and issue one of five enforcement decisions (allow, deny, modify, defer or step up), with each decision generating a signed, tamper-evident receipt auditors can verify independently.

Airia will demonstrate AARM-conformant enforcement at Black Hat USA 2026 (Booth 4511, Aug 3-6, Las Vegas). As AI agents proliferate across enterprise environments, verifiable runtime security standards like AARM are becoming a key basis for procurement and compliance decisions.

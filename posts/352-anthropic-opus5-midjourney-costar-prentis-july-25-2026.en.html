⚡ July 25 Tech Roundup: Anthropic Launches Claude Opus 5 at Half the Price of Fable, Midjourney Makes First Acquisition of Astrology App Co-Star, Reid Hoffman and Marc Pincus Co-Found Prentis AI Lab

July 24-25, 2026 brings a wave of major AI industry news: Anthropic launches the next-generation Claude Opus 5 with near-flagship Fable 5 performance at half the cost; AI image generation leader Midjourney completes its first acquisition, buying astrology app Co-Star with over 30 million registered users; and LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman teams up with Zynga founder Marc Pincus to launch Prentis, an AI lab valued at $1 billion raising $100 million to automate desktop computer tasks.

1. Anthropic Releases Claude Opus 5: Near Fable 5 Performance at Half the Price

At 10:00 AM PDT on July 24, Anthropic officially launched Claude Opus 5 — the first Opus model to enter its fifth generation. Positioned as "the best choice for daily use," the model delivers strong performance in coding, knowledge work, and agentic tasks.

Positioning: Anthropic says Opus 5 approaches the capabilities of its flagship Claude Fable 5 across many domains, but at half the price — $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens, the same pricing as Opus 4.8. This pricing strategy directly targets increasingly cost-conscious enterprise customers.

Performance Highlights

  • Coding & Knowledge Work: Opus 5 outperforms Fable 5 on complex coding and knowledge-intensive tasks, making it Anthropic's strongest knowledge work model
  • Fast Mode: A new high-speed inference mode delivering approximately 2.5x faster response at 2x the base price
  • Tool Chain Optimization: Supports mid-conversation tool changes without cache invalidation — highly beneficial for agent developers
  • No Data Retention: Continues Opus policy of no data retention requirements for general access
  • Safety Boundaries: Remains behind the restricted Mythos 5 model on dual-use capabilities like offensive cybersecurity and biological research

Deployment: Opus 5 is available immediately on all platforms, becoming the default model on Claude Max and the strongest model on Claude Pro. Developers can access it via the claude-opus-5 API identifier. AWS Bedrock has also rolled it out across North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific regions.

This launch sends a clear signal: the AI race is shifting from pure "capability competition" to a "cost-efficiency battle." With Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and multiple Chinese startups steadily releasing cheaper alternatives, Anthropic chose to strengthen its mid-to-high-end market position with Opus 5 while keeping Fable 5 as the frontier flagship.

2. Midjourney's First Acquisition: Astrology App Co-Star for Consumer Market Entry

On July 24, Midjourney — the AI image generation powerhouse — announced its first-ever acquisition: Co-Star, a popular astrology app with over 30 million registered users (approximately 4.3 million monthly active users). Deal terms were not disclosed.

Acquisition Rationale: Midjourney founder David Holz revealed he met Co-Star founder Banu Guler over five years ago, and the two have long discussed "how computers can reveal our humanity." Co-Star will continue operating under Guler's full control, while Guler also takes on the role of Midjourney's Chief Design Officer. Her team of approximately two dozen people joins Midjourney.

Strategic Significance

  • Breaking Discord Dependence: Midjourney has primarily operated through Discord and web interfaces, lacking a standalone mobile app. Co-Star brings mature mobile product capabilities and a young user base (average age 18-25)
  • Consumer Expansion: Following its recent entry into medical imaging, Midjourney is building more consumer products. Co-Star's product design, social interaction, and subscription monetization experience directly support this pivot
  • Financial Strength: Midjourney, which has rejected outside VC funding and operates profitably with ~$500M annual revenue, made the acquisition with cash
Co-Star CEO Banu Guler wrote on X: "At a time when it feels like many labs are pushing toward replacing humans with computers, David and I are taking a different approach. We share the belief that computers can be tools that reveal our humanity to ourselves."

Co-Star was founded in 2017, combining NASA data, human astrologers, and AI to deliver personalized horoscopes. It raised a $5.2M seed round in 2019 and a Series A led by Spark Capital in 2021.

3. Prentis AI Lab: Reid Hoffman and Marc Pincus Co-Found New AI Research Lab, Raising $100M

According to an exclusive TechCrunch report, LinkedIn co-founder and Greylock partner Reid Hoffman has teamed up with Zynga founder Marc Pincus to launch a new AI research lab called Prentis. The company is in talks to raise $100 million at a $1 billion valuation.

Positioning: Unlike the first wave of AI startups focused on code generation, Prentis aims to automate broader digital work — the repetitive desktop tasks that drain knowledge workers' time daily. The company describes itself as a "neolab," straddling the line between a research institution and a product company.

Team & Background

  • Serial entrepreneur Ritankar Das serves as CEO and co-founder
  • Reid Hoffman recently stepped down from Microsoft's board to go "founder mode," backing multiple AI ventures including Manas AI (AI drug discovery)
  • Marc Pincus published his memoir "Life at the Speed of Play" last month and co-runs Reinvent Capital with Hoffman

Hoffman and Pincus's bet is clear and bold: AI's biggest impact won't come from helping 30 million developers write better code, but from liberating over 1 billion knowledge workers trapped in digital busywork. If successful, the $100M raise will provide Prentis with substantial runway to build foundational models, recruit top talent, and establish enterprise partnerships.

Competition in this space is formidable: Microsoft, Google, and numerous well-funded startups are all targeting the same "desktop automation" space. Prentis will need to prove its product and business model quickly.

Market Implications

These three developments reflect key trends reshaping the AI landscape:

  • Cost efficiency is the new battleground: Anthropic Opus 5's half-price near-flagship positioning shows the shift from benchmark racing to cost competition
  • AI companies go consumer: Midjourney's Co-Star acquisition marks a move from pure technology play to direct-to-consumer product strategy
  • Top Silicon Valley talent goes all-in on AI: Hoffman leaving Microsoft's board, Pincus pivoting from gaming — the most successful entrepreneurs see AI as a generational opportunity

With Kimi K3's open-weight release scheduled for July 27 and both Anthropic and OpenAI advancing toward IPOs, the competitive dynamics of the AI industry heading into H2 2026 are becoming increasingly intense and diverse.

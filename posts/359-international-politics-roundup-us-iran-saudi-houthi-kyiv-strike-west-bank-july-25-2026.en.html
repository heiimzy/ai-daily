⚡ July 25 International Politics Roundup: Saudi Strikes Houthis for First Time in Years, US-Iran 13th Night of Strikes with Trump "Locked and Loaded", Russia Daytime Strike Kills 10 at Kyiv Defense Exhibition, Israel Raids 70 Homes in West Bank

🇸🇦 Saudi-Led Coalition Strikes Houthi Targets in Hodeidah: First Military Action Since 2022 Truce

The Saudi-led coalition launched airstrikes on Houthi-controlled Hodeidah in Yemen late Friday, targeting telecommunications facilities and a military camp on Kamaran Island — the first direct Saudi military action against the Iran-backed group since a 2022 ceasefire.

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki described the strikes as a "decisive and strong response" to Houthi threats against commercial shipping in the Red Sea. Houthi-run Al-Masirah TV reported that strikes hit the telecommunications building near the eastern gate of Hodeidah port and the naval base at Ras al-Kathib, injuring a worker and a woman.

The escalation follows the Houthis' Monday announcement of a maritime blockade on Saudi Arabia and Thursday's attacks on two Saudi oil tankers in the Red Sea. Saudi state media confirmed the NCC MASA (Encelia) tanker was hit, causing a fire at its bow, with all crew safe. The Houthis' Political Bureau warned the strikes "will not pass without a response," declaring: "The blockade will be met with a blockade, ports with ports, and escalation with escalation."

The Red Sea route has become Saudi Arabia's critical export lifeline after Iran effectively shut down the Strait of Hormuz, forcing Riyadh to divert millions of barrels of oil daily via its east-west pipeline to Red Sea ports.

🇺🇸🇮🇷 US-Iran War: 13th Night of Strikes as Trump Says 'Locked and Loaded'

US Central Command completed its 13th consecutive night of strikes against Iran early Friday, targeting military command centers, drone storage facilities, communication networks, coastal surveillance sites, and maritime capabilities. The two-hour operation struck Qeshm Island, Bandar Abbas, Ahvaz, Jask, Konarak, and other locations across southern and central Iran.

President Donald Trump, speaking at the White House Correspondents' Association Dinner on Friday, confirmed the US is in talks with Iran, saying Tehran is getting "more and more serious." However, he emphasized that US forces are "locked and loaded and ready to go" if needed. "We're talking to them, so I think while we're talking, we'll see what comes of it," Trump said, adding he had not yet decided on launching major strikes.

US forces disabled the M/T Lavine merchant vessel in the Gulf of Oman after its crew attempted to breach the blockade on Iranian ports at least four times. CENTCOM spokesman Captain Tim Hawkins said US forces "fired into its engine room" after repeated warnings were ignored. This is the second commercial ship disabled since the blockade was reimposed on July 13.

Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed attacks on the US base in Udairi, Kuwait, destroying three ammunition sheds, and a watchtower for the US Navy's Fifth Fleet in Bahrain. Both Bahrain and Jordan reported intercepting Iranian aerial attacks on Friday. Iran also claimed to have destroyed a Patriot air defense system and a reconnaissance balloon in Erbil, Iraq. Sirens sounded in Bahrain Saturday morning, with the interior ministry urging citizens to head to the nearest safe place.

US and UK Plan International Coalition for Strait of Hormuz

According to Axios, the US and UK are planning a high-level meeting in London next week to form an international coalition to protect commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. Washington wants allies to send de-mining vessels, naval vessels, and drones to safeguard the waterway. A European diplomat told Axios the effort is tied to a US exit strategy from the Iran conflict: "The Americans are looking for a way out and they want our help."

🇷🇺🇺🇦 Russia Kills 10 in Rare Daytime Ballistic Missile Strike on Kyiv Defense Exhibition

Russia launched a rare daytime ballistic missile attack on Kyiv on Friday at around 11:30 AM local time. The Ukrainian Air Force reported three Iskander-M/S-400 ballistic missiles were fired from the north; one was intercepted, while two struck a private training ground in Bucha district where a defense industry exhibition was taking place.

Ukrainian Prosecutor General Ruslan Kravchenko confirmed at least 10 people were killed and approximately 100 wounded in the attack. President Volodymyr Zelensky initially reported six dead, with the toll later rising. The Ukrainian Defense Industry Council confirmed the site was hosting representatives from Ukraine's defense sector.

Yuriy Ihnat, head of communications for the Ukrainian Air Force Command, said preliminary information indicated Russia used Iskander-M and S-400 missiles. He urged Ukrainians to remain vigilant, warning that Russia has stockpiled significant missile reserves and could launch a large-scale attack.

In his evening address, Zelensky warned that Russian intelligence indicates Moscow is preparing a "massive missile attack" within the next 48 hours. He also confirmed that Ukraine is preparing a meeting with President Trump and his team in Washington.

Zelensky Preparing Meeting with Trump in Washington

Zelensky confirmed late Friday that Ukraine is "preparing for a meeting with the President of the United States and his team." The White House confirmed that Trump and Zelensky are scheduled to meet in Washington in the coming days. According to Ukrainian diplomatic sources cited by Suspilne, Zelensky is expected to visit the US on Sunday, July 28, attend Senator Lindsey Graham's funeral, and hold bilateral talks with Trump. Zelensky also stated that a trilateral meeting involving Ukraine, the US, and Russia could take place before autumn.

🇮🇱 Israeli Forces Raid 70 Homes in Nablus, Arrest 50 Palestinians

Israeli forces launched a large-scale military operation in the Nablus area of the occupied West Bank overnight Friday, raiding approximately 70 homes and arresting around 50 Palestinians. Al Jazeera reported this was one of the most extensive operations in the region in recent months. Nablus Governor Ghassan Daghlas reported that six Palestinians had been killed and dozens more wounded or detained in the past two days, describing the events as "real aggression."

Settler violence is also escalating. Four Palestinians were killed near Nablus in overnight funerals held Friday. Human Rights Watch warned that the surge in settler violence threatens mass atrocities, calling for international action to end the Israeli government's role in escalating reprisals across the West Bank.

Separately, UNESCO inscribed a major archaeological site in the West Bank and a group of castles in southern Lebanon on the World Heritage list, over Israel's objections.

🌊 South China Sea: China Water Cannons Philippine Vessels for Second Consecutive Day

Chinese Coast Guard vessels again fired water cannons at Philippine government ships near Scarborough Shoal (Huangyan Island) on Friday, the third confrontation this week. The PCG reported CCG vessel 3107 fired at BRP Cape San Agustin at 6:26 AM, followed by CCG 21581 attacking BRP Datu Paduhinog at 7:35 AM, hitting it directly three times. By 9:30 AM, 26 vessels were in the area: 10 Philippine ships vs 12 CCG and 3 Chinese maritime militia vessels.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio confirmed a US Coast Guard vessel intervened during a July 20 incident at Ayungin Shoal to help rescue an injured Philippine Navy personnel. The US condemned China's "dangerous" and "aggressive" actions.

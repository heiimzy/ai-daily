⚡ July 27 Tech Roundup: Tencent Revives QQ Pet with AI — Iconic Digital Pet Returns After 8 Years with Hunyuan Hy3; Meituan Open-Sources 1.6T Parameter LongCat-2.0; Xiaomi Pengcheng SUV Launch July 30

1. Tencent Brings Back QQ Pet with AI: Hunyuan Hy3-Powered Emotional Companion

On July 27, Tencent QQ officially announced the revival of QQ Pet — the iconic digital penguin that defined a generation of Chinese internet users. After being retired in 2018, the virtual pet is now returning to users who can adopt it by searching "QQ Pet" within the mobile QQ app.

Unlike the original version that relied on scripted interactions, the new QQ Pet is powered by Tencent's Hunyuan Hy3 large language model, giving it genuine conversational AI capabilities. Users can choose from different personality templates — "Little Sun," "Clingy," "Mischievous," "Drama Queen" — and the pet's tone, behavior, and responses adapt accordingly.

The pet can proactively greet users, remind them about weather, express needs, share experiences from its "work" and "adventures," and generate daily "pet diaries" based on accumulated interactions. It now features full 3D visuals, including penguin, dog, and garlic-goose avatars, with realistic touch feedback on fur and movement.

Most notably, QQ Pet is no longer confined to a separate window. It can appear directly in chat threads, jumping around, interacting with friends' pets, making the AI character a true part of daily social interactions. This integration leverages QQ's massive social graph — still one of China's largest messaging platforms.

The return of QQ Pet is widely seen as Tencent's strategic move into AI-powered social products. Rather than building a new AI companion from scratch, Tencent is reviving an IP with deep emotional resonance among Chinese users. However, the AI emotional companion space is already crowded with global players like Character.AI and Replika, and QQ Pet will need to differentiate through QQ's unique social graph advantages.

2. Meituan Open-Sources LongCat-2.0: A 1.6-Trillion Parameter Model Built for Agentic Coding on Domestic Chips

Meituan has officially open-sourced LongCat-2.0, a massive 1.6-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model. Activating approximately 48 billion parameters per inference, the model natively supports a 1-million-token context window and is specifically designed for agentic coding tasks in real-world business scenarios.

Architecturally, LongCat-2.0 introduces innovative LongCat sparse attention and N-gram Embedding mechanisms, enhancing long-context processing efficiency and token-level representation. Combined with dynamic activation mechanisms, the model delivers strong performance on code generation and agentic benchmarks.

A key differentiator is LongCat-2.0's native support for domestic Chinese AI chips. Meituan has released inference code optimized for platforms like Huawei Ascend and Cambricon, enabling developers to deploy trillion-parameter models entirely on domestic hardware — a significant contrast to many open-source models optimized exclusively for NVIDIA GPUs.

The complete model weights and code are available through official channels under an open-source license permitting free download, deployment, and commercial use. Industry analysts view this as a strategic move: by open-sourcing its core AI capability and actively supporting domestic chips, Meituan is contributing to building China's sovereign AI infrastructure.

3. Xiaomi Announces Pengcheng SUV Launch Event: N90 and N70 Coming July 30

Lei Jun, founder and CEO of Xiaomi, officially announced on Weibo that Xiaomi's second technical launch event will take place on July 30 at 7 PM Beijing time, unveiling the Pengcheng series — the company's highly anticipated entry into the SUV market.

The Pengcheng N90 and N70 are positioned as extended-range electric SUVs featuring "intelligent variable large space" design. This marks Xiaomi's second automotive technology presentation following the successful launch of the SU7 sedan. Lei Jun touted the new models' ultra-low maintenance requirements — only needing service every 3 years or 30,000 km after the first check — and emphasized competitive fuel consumption and ownership costs.

With the SU7 already making inroads in China's competitive EV sedan market, the Pengcheng SUV series represents a critical expansion of Xiaomi's automotive product line into the high-growth SUV segment. The July 30 event is expected to reveal detailed specifications, pricing strategy, and delivery timelines.

4. Tencent Hunyuan Hy3 Official Version Launches: Free Agent Features, API Call Volume Surges 68x

Tencent has officially released the production version of its Hunyuan Hy3 (混元3) foundational model, an iterative upgrade from the Hy3 preview launched in April. The new version significantly enhances agentic capabilities with reduced hallucination rates across all task categories.

Under the leadership of newly appointed Chief AI Scientist Yao Shunyu, Tencent increased post-training compute scale and data quality. Hy3 is available via Tencent Cloud API, while the Yuanbao app has integrated Hy3 with a completely free Agent feature.

Users can now delegate complex multi-step tasks to the AI — planning trips, compiling research reports, or executing cross-application workflows — all through natural language. Within one week of launch, Hy3's total API call volume surged 68x compared to its predecessor Hy2, topping OpenRouter's global LLM leaderboard by call count.

Tencent's strategy is pragmatic: rather than racing for model parameter supremacy, the company is embedding AI capabilities into products users already engage with daily. From QQ Pet to Yuanbao Agent, Tencent is building a comprehensive AI ecosystem spanning social, productivity, and content consumption.

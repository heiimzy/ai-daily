⚡ Aug 4 Finance Update: onsemi Beats Guidance With AI Data Center Revenue Set to Double, Vertex Posts $3.33B Beat and Raises Outlook, Diamondback EPS Hits $6.65 With Buyback Doubled to $16B, Bitcoin Back Above $63.8K

In the early hours of August 4 Beijing time, Monday's after-hours earnings wave took center stage: chipmaker onsemi delivered a Q2 report that beat the midpoint of its guidance across the board and said AI data center revenue is set to more than double this year; pharma giant Vertex beat revenue estimates and raised its full-year outlook; shale leader Diamondback Energy crushed EPS estimates on strong oil prices and doubled its buyback authorization to $16 billion. Meanwhile, Bitcoin climbed back above $63,800 as US stocks surged, signaling a clear improvement in risk appetite. Here is this finance roundup.

1. onsemi Q2: Beat Across the Board, AI Data Center Revenue Expected to More Than Double in 2026

After Monday's close (around 4:54 p.m. ET), onsemi (Nasdaq: ON) reported second-quarter 2026 results with revenue, gross margin and earnings per share all above the midpoint of its guidance range. CEO Hassane El-Khoury said: "We delivered revenue, gross margin and earnings per share above the midpoint of guidance, reflecting strengthening demand, particularly across AI-driven applications, and growing customer adoption of our differentiated solutions, including Treo and our high voltage power solutions." He stressed that "AI data center remains our fastest-growing business, and we now expect revenue to more than double in 2026," underscoring the strength of the company's intelligent power portfolio across the "power tree."

The efficiency metrics were equally striking: CFO Thad Trent noted that year-over-year EPS grew four times faster than revenue, driven by gross margin expansion and disciplined cost management, and that free cash flow margin expanded from approximately 7% to 27% year over year. The company hosted its earnings call at 5:00 p.m. ET. As a key supplier to the automotive and industrial semiconductor markets, onsemi's beat adds fresh evidence to the narrative that AI demand is spilling over into power semiconductors.

2. Vertex Q2: Revenue of $3.33B Beats, Full-Year Guidance Raised

After Monday's close, Vertex Pharmaceuticals (Nasdaq: VRTX) reported second-quarter 2026 results: total revenue of $3.33 billion, up 12% year over year, roughly 4.6% above the analyst consensus of $3.19 billion; non-GAAP EPS of $4.73 came in roughly in line with estimates (versus $4.52 a year ago). US revenue rose 11% to $2.06 billion, driven by strong cystic fibrosis (CF) patient demand, new ALYFTREK initiations and contributions from CASGEVY and JOURNAVX; international revenue grew 14% to $1.28 billion.

The company raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $13.1-13.2 billion (from $12.95-13.1 billion) and continues to expect $500 million or more in revenue from non-CF products, namely CASGEVY gene therapy and JOURNAVX non-opioid pain treatment. Cash, equivalents and marketable securities stood at $13.6 billion at quarter-end. The stock was roughly flat at $469.26 immediately after the print — after a ~10% pullback over the past month, the "beat and raise" was read as positive confirmation of the company's diversification strategy.

3. Diamondback Energy: Oil Prices Drive EPS of $6.65, Buyback Authorization Doubled to $16B

After Monday's close (around 4:01 p.m. ET), Diamondback Energy (Nasdaq: FANG) reported Q2 results: total revenue of $5.56 billion, up roughly 51% year over year (from $3.68 billion); net income of $1.88 billion, or diluted EPS of $6.65, well above the consensus of about $6.08-6.14 (versus $2.38 a year ago); adjusted EBITDA of $3.94 billion and free cash flow of $2.33 billion on cash capex of just $996 million.

Production averaged 1.018 million barrels of oil equivalent per day, surpassing the one-million-BOE milestone, including 525,000 barrels per day of oil; realized oil prices jumped 53% year over year to $96.82 per barrel, while realized natural gas prices fell to negative $2.15 per Mcf. The company cut total debt to $12.77 billion (net debt of $12.30 billion), declared a $1.10 per share base dividend, repurchased 756,385 shares for $141 million, and doubled its share repurchase authorization to $16.0 billion (about $9.9 billion remaining). Management also raised full-year 2026 oil production guidance to 522+ MBO/d while keeping the ~$3.9 billion capital budget unchanged — a direct expression of the shareholder-returns-first strategy.

4. Crypto: Bitcoin Climbs Back Above $63,800 as Stocks Surge

In contrast to last week's weakness, Bitcoin rebounded strongly on Monday (US time, August 3) alongside the equity rally: CoinDesk live data showed BTC trading near $63,800-63,900 in the early hours of August 4 Beijing time, up roughly 0.6%-1.3% over 24 hours, with Ethereum recovering to about $1,870. CoinDesk's live blog noted Bitcoin "flirted with the $64,000 level for most of the afternoon" after defending support near $62,600 in the morning.

On the macro side, the S&P 500 posted its best start to a month in nearly four years (per Bespoke, its strongest monthly opening day since October 2022), lifting overall risk appetite. Crypto-related equities rallied too — Galaxy Digital, Bullish and Robinhood gained 4%-5%, while Coinbase and Strategy (MicroStrategy) added about 2%. Headwinds remain: spot Bitcoin ETFs saw roughly $61.5 million in net outflows last week (ending three straight weeks of inflows), the CLARITY Act was not placed on Monday's Senate agenda, and the Coldcard hardware wallet attack story continues to develop. Technically, the $62,600-63,000 support zone held multiple tests on Monday; whether BTC can reclaim $64,000 will likely determine the near-term direction.

5. The Week Ahead: AMD and SpaceX Earnings, Friday's Jobs Report

The earnings-plus-data week rolls on: on Tuesday (August 4), AMD and SpaceX (its first quarterly report since going public) report after the close, offering fresh reads on AI data center chip demand and commercial space economics, with McDonald's, Costco and Disney also due. On the data front, Tuesday's June JOLTS job openings and Wednesday's ADP private payrolls set the stage for Friday's (August 7) July US employment report — a Reuters poll points to roughly 83,000-88,000 jobs added and a 4.3% unemployment rate. Rate futures currently price about a 65% probability of a September Fed hike (the funds rate stands at 3.50%-3.75%); if the jobs data and the August 12 CPI print reinforce inflation stickiness, market volatility could pick up further.

From onsemi and Vertex to Diamondback, this after-hours earnings wave is another reminder that under the twin themes of "AI capex" and "high oil prices," the quality of corporate earnings is being scrutinized line by line — a revenue beat is only the entry ticket; cash flow quality and shareholder returns are where pricing power is won.

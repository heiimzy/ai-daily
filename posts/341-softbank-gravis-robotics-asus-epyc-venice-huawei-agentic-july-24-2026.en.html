SoftBank in Talks to Acquire Swiss Robotics Startup Gravis for Over $500 Million

SoftBank Group Corp. is considering an acquisition of Zurich-based Gravis Robotics AG, people familiar with the matter told Bloomberg on Thursday, in a deal that could ultimately value the startup at more than $500 million. The Japanese tech conglomerate plans to hold the stake through a newly established company called Roze, potentially executing the transaction in stages by acquiring existing shares and injecting fresh capital over time.

Founded in 2022 as a spinout from ETH Zurich, Gravis develops hardware and software that enables earthmoving equipment such as excavators and bulldozers to operate autonomously. The company raised $23 million in venture funding in November 2025. A deal would complement SoftBank's existing robotics portfolio, which includes the pending $5.375 billion acquisition of ABB's industrial robotics business and the newly formed Robo HD holding company that consolidates stakes in roughly 20 robotics ventures including Berkshire Grey, Agile Robots, and Skild AI.

SoftBank founder Masayoshi Son has been rebuilding the company's robotics ambition after earlier ventures like Pepper (halted in 2020) and Boston Dynamics (sold to Hyundai in 2020). The Roze entity is being prepared for a US listing, Bloomberg reported in April. While the Gravis deal would add outdoor autonomous construction machinery to SoftBank's factory automation focus from ABB, the sources cautioned that no final decisions on size or structure have been made.

ASUS Unveils AI Inference Servers Powered by AMD 6th-Gen EPYC Venice Processors

ASUS on Thursday announced a new server lineup powered by AMD's 6th-gen EPYC 9006 series processors (Venice architecture) at an event in San Francisco, marking the first major OEM deployment of AMD's latest server CPU for enterprise AI workloads. The announcement came alongside AMD's Advancing AI 2026 conference where the Venice chip was formally launched.

The portfolio features two series: the flagship dual-socket RS700A/720A for extreme compute density with 32 DIMM slots, PCIe 6.0, and up to 32 E3.S storage bays in a 2U form factor; and the single-socket RS500A/520A optimized for space-constrained environments with a sub-800mm depth. Both integrate ASUS proprietary thermal management (Thermal Radar 3.0 with PID Control), DIMM.2 smart storage repositioning, and DC-MHS modular architecture for rapid serviceability.

The Venice processor — built on TSMC's 2nm process with up to 256 Zen 6 cores and 512 threads — was positioned by AMD CEO Lisa Su as a direct competitor to Nvidia's Vera server CPU. ASUS stated the new servers are engineered specifically for AI inferencing, cloud virtualization, and business-critical workloads, offering enterprises a scalable foundation for AI deployment from data center to edge.

Huawei Cloud Launches Agentic Infrastructure in Thailand for Enterprise AI Agents

Huawei Cloud on Thursday held its "Huawei Cloud Summit Thailand 2026" in Bangkok, unveiling "Huawei Cloud Agentic Infrastructure" — a comprehensive platform designed for developing and deploying AI agents — and launching the CodeArts Agent Open Beta Test in the country. This marks Huawei Cloud's first systematic AI agent infrastructure rollout in the Southeast Asian market.

Agentic Infrastructure comprises four core components: UnifiedBus-based AI Cluster Service (AICS) for efficient token generation; Agentic Memory Storage Service (AMS) delivering PB-scale memory to address agent memory bottlenecks for long-horizon tasks; AgentSphere providing a secure autonomous runtime environment; and CCE VolcanoNext enabling unified scheduling of general-purpose and AI computing resources.

CodeArts Agent combines IDE functionality with autonomous development capabilities and coding models, supporting project-level code generation, code completion, R&D knowledge Q&A, and unit test generation. A new "Agent Team" mode can automatically form a development team with multiple AI agents collaborating concurrently. Huawei Cloud also upgraded its security services with model lifecycle protection spanning AI infrastructure, training data, model inference, and agent applications, plus Data Capsule technology ensuring data can only be used within authorized environments.

South Korea's Nota AI Joins AMD Robotics Partner Network

South Korean AI model compression and optimization specialist Nota AI announced Thursday at AMD Advancing AI 2026 that it has joined the AMD Robotics Partner Network, extending its on-device AI optimization technology into the robotics sector.

Nota AI's technology enables efficient AI model deployment on resource-constrained robotic hardware. Through the partnership, Nota AI will optimize model size, computational workload, and memory usage for AMD processors and robotic system environments. The company previously deployed on-device AI solutions across smart city, industrial safety, and mobility applications. Its entry into the AMD Robotics Partner Network is expected to accelerate the deployment of physical AI solutions across industrial environments.

⚡ Aug 2 Tech News: iPhone 18 Pro OLED Mass Production Kicks Off with Samsung and LG, Kimi K3 Open Source Fuels China's Super-Node Rally, China's First Brain-Computer Interface Prescription

Three fronts advanced at once in the tech world on the morning of August 2: Samsung Display and LG Display have simultaneously kicked off mass production of OLED panels for the iPhone 18 Pro series - Samsung at 64% and LG at 36% as exclusive suppliers, with BOE missing out due to yield shortfalls; China Galaxy Securities issued a bullish note on Kimi K3's open source, arguing that the model "saturated the cluster within 48 hours and forced a pause on new C-end subscriptions," validating an AI compute crunch and catalyzing the domestic super-node supply chain; and China's first brain-computer interface (BCI) prescription was issued in Shanghai, with the domestic implantable NEO system completing its first-ever surgery worldwide - from phone displays to AI compute to medical technology, the broader tech track is firing on all cylinders.

iPhone 18 Pro OLED Production Begins: Samsung and LG Sole Suppliers, BOE Missing from High-End Orders

According to South Korean media outlet ETNEWS, Samsung Display and LG Display have started mass production of the full OLED panel sets for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, with the screen orders exclusively split between the two Korean makers: Samsung at 64% and LG at 36%, while Chinese panel maker BOE is absent from this high-end program. BOE had secured high-end OLED supply on the iPhone 17 Pro but failed to extend that into the iPhone 18 Pro series - supply-chain sources attribute the loss mainly to a yield gap in next-generation LTPO+ panels: BOE's LTPO+ yield is roughly 65% to 70%, below Apple's requirement of 85% or higher, with quality consistency and large-scale delivery capability still trailing the Korean vendors.

Per the roadmap, the iPhone 18 Pro series is set to launch in September alongside Apple's first foldable iPhone, while the standard iPhone 18 will be delayed to Q1 2027 - a likely "Pro first, standard later" staggered cadence going forward. The foldable iPhone (reportedly named iPhone Ultra) will source its flexible screens exclusively from Samsung Display under a three-year exclusive supply agreement, with an initial order of roughly 3 million panels and yields already above 80%. On the spec side, the iPhone 18 Pro series is expected to carry the A20 Pro flagship chip built on TSMC's 2nm process, a new variable-aperture main camera (f/1.4 to f/2.8), a 24MP autofocus front camera, and Apple's in-house C2 modem with satellite data connectivity.

Samsung Display and LG Display have started iPhone 18 Pro OLED mass production as exclusive suppliers - 64% Samsung, 36% LG - while BOE misses out with LTPO+ yields of roughly 65-70%, below Apple's 85% threshold.

Kimi K3 Open Source Fuels Compute Rally: Cluster Saturated in 48 Hours, Galaxy Securities Backs Super Nodes

China Galaxy Securities issued a research note saying Kimi K3 saturated the existing cluster within just 48 hours of launch, prompting Moonshot AI to suspend new C-end member subscriptions - validating both the model's capability and the reality of a compute crunch. The brokerage argues Kimi K3 is not suppressing compute demand: on one hand, Chinese open-source models are closing in on the world's top closed-source models, forcing leading labs to train at larger scale and iterate faster to keep differentiation; on the other, inference demand keeps expanding - Kimi K3 has already achieved Day-0 adaptation with multiple domestic compute platforms, lifting demand for domestic super nodes and, in turn, servers, switches, optical modules, liquid cooling and power supply along the domestic compute supply chain - the note recommends watching super-node and related supply-chain names.

The centerpiece of this rally: Kimi K3 is a 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts (MoE) model officially open-sourced by Moonshot AI on July 27, with native vision understanding, a 1-million-token context window, and three training-infrastructure technologies - MoonEP, FlashKDA and AgentEnv - released alongside. It is the first open-source model to reach the 2.8T parameter scale, topped Hugging Face's trending list on release day with 4,000+ likes in 30 minutes, and is widely seen as another heavyweight in China's open-source ecosystem after DeepSeek. From Bloomberg's report on Kimi K3 training on 20,000 Nvidia chips via Alibaba to today's broker coverage, the market is rapidly repricing Moonshot AI's commercial and industrial value.

China's First Brain-Computer Interface Prescription: Domestic NEO Completes First-Ever Implant Surgery

According to the Jinan municipal government website, the first prescription for the implantable NEO hand-motor function compensation system - approved for market - was recently issued in Shanghai, with the world's first implant surgery completed in tandem. From securing the global "first approval certificate" on March 13 to writing the global "first prescription" on July 13, this domestic BCI device took just four months to leap from R&D to clinical application. Shanghai authorities accelerated approval guidance with a research-review-linked process, while hospitals completed price filing, device admission and patient screening in parallel - paving the way for scaled clinical rollout.

The NEO milestone means patients with hand-motor dysfunction caused by spinal cord injury or stroke sequelae may gain a path to hand-function compensation and reconstruction through an implantable brain-computer interface. It is also a key global marker for the BCI industry's transition from laboratory to prescription-based clinical practice - with the "first certificate, first prescription, first surgery" chain now complete, China's high-end medical devices are rapidly showing competitiveness on the frontier track of neural modulation.

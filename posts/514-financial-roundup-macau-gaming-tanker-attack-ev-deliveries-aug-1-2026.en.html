⚡ August 1 Finance News: Macau July Gaming Revenue Misses Forecasts, Tanker Hit Off Oman Adds Energy Risk, Leapmotor First to Top 100K Monthly EV Deliveries

Macau July Gaming Revenue at MOP 20.26 Billion, Down 8.4% and Missing Estimates

Data released by Macau's Gaming Inspection and Coordination Bureau (DICJ) on Saturday (August 1) showed gross gaming revenue of MOP 20.26 billion in July, down 8.4% year-on-year and slightly below the 6.2% decline expected by the market; January-July gross gaming revenue reached MOP 147.16 billion, up 4.4% year-on-year (Wall Street CN flash, 12:48 Beijing time). Despite the peak summer tourism season, the monthly figure turned negative on a year-on-year basis, underscoring persistent pressure on Macau's casino industry from volatile high-end gaming demand and mainland visitor spending. The cumulative growth through the first seven months remains positive, suggesting the sector is still on a recovery track — but analysts note that intensifying regional competition and softer macro consumption are eroding the sector's resilience. Gaming stocks are historically highly sensitive to revenue prints, so the data will prompt fresh repricing of mid-year earnings expectations for related names.

Tanker Attacked Off Oman: UKMTO Says Engine Room Damaged, Vessel Adrift

The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) reported on August 1 that a tanker was attacked about 11 nautical miles northeast of Liwa, Oman — the vessel reported being struck by an "unknown projectile," sustaining engine-room damage, and is currently adrift. Local coast guard authorities have been informed, with no reports of casualties or environmental pollution so far (CCTV News via Wall Street CN, 12:51 Beijing time). The attack comes against an already fraught backdrop: over the past week, transit risk through the Strait of Hormuz has escalated sharply amid the US-Iran conflict, with US Central Command saying it had rerouted 30 commercial vessels and allowed nearly 30 ships through the blockade line. The tanker incident, layered on top of strait-disruption worries, is pushing shipping insurance and energy risk premiums higher again — Brent crude had already gained more than 20% in July, its strongest monthly performance since March. As long as maritime security in the Middle East remains unsettled, analysts say, volatility in oil and shipping markets will be hard to contain, with OPEC+ supply policy the next key directional variable in August.

Chinese EV Startups' July Delivery Scorecard: Leapmotor First to Break 100K, NIO and XPeng Post Double-Digit Growth

Several Chinese EV startups released July delivery numbers on the morning of August 1: Leapmotor delivered 101,267 units, up 102% year-on-year, becoming the first domestic new-energy startup to surpass 100,000 deliveries in a single month (Wall Street CN flash, 11:08 Beijing time); XPeng delivered 38,027 vehicles in July (12:02 Beijing time); NIO delivered 35,934 units, up 71.0% year-on-year — comprising 20,008 NIO-brand cars (+57.9%), 10,155 Onvo units (+69.9%) and 5,771 firefly units (+143.9%) (12:16 Beijing time); and Zeekr delivered 35,837 vehicles, up 111% year-on-year to a record high (11:07 Beijing time). Overall, with trade-in subsidy policies continuing and new models ramping up, the new-energy vehicle market maintained strong momentum in July. Leapmotor's explosive growth is particularly striking — crossing the 100K monthly mark puts it firmly in the first tier and injects new variables into the industry's price war and capacity competition.

Bonds & FX: 10-Year Treasury Yield at Highest Since January Last Year, Dollar Bullish Bets at Highest Since 2014

In late New York trading on Friday (July 31), the benchmark US 10-year Treasury yield rose 6.15 basis points to 4.7347%, ending July up more than 25 basis points for the month — the highest level since January last year (Wall Street CN, updated 06:46 Beijing time August 1). Meanwhile, CFTC data showed net non-commercial bullish dollar positioning rising to $49.23 billion in the week ended July 28, the highest since 2014 — positioning built before the Fed's July rate decision, amid sharp dollar swings and swirling FX-intervention rumors. The combination of elevated Treasury yields and a firm dollar continues to pressure global risk assets and emerging markets. Next week's US July nonfarm payrolls report will directly test how the Federal Reserve navigates its internal rate-hike rift — the next key data set markets are watching.

📡 Massive Xbox Outage Exceeds 20 Hours, Blocks Disc-Based Games Too

A major Xbox service outage that began Sunday evening has stretched into Monday night — and the impact has proven far more severe than initially expected, even preventing users from playing physical disc-based games.

According to The Verge, Xbox's status page first reported the outage at around 11 PM ET on Sunday. Since then, users have been unable to sign in, launch apps, or access the Xbox store. By Monday morning, the status page added that users "may have problems" playing both digital and disc-based games, which social media posts quickly confirmed. Attempts to launch Game Pass titles triggered error code 0x87e107df, indicating licenses could not be verified.

Xbox Support posted on X that "our teams are focused on multiple investigation angles and remain hard at work," later adding that "things are slowly improving." However, users continued reporting issues with digital and backward-compatible games through Monday evening.

The outage comes as Xbox has been testing a "disc-to-digital" feature that would grant users digital copies of games they own on disc. This incident highlights the potential downside of a system that ties authentication requirements even to physical copies of games.

The disruption follows major layoffs at Xbox announced earlier this month, further fueling community concerns about service reliability.

⚖️ Apple Sued Over Fake Sparrow Wallet App That Cost Users $1.8M

Apple is facing a new lawsuit from three customers who collectively lost more than $1.8 million after downloading a fraudulent Sparrow Wallet Bitcoin app from the App Store.

According to the complaint filed July 24 in the U.S. District Court of Northern California, plaintiff James Ramirez lost approximately $875,000, Christopher Ellis lost around $840,000, and Jalen Delgado lost roughly $120,000. Between May and August 2025, each downloaded a fake Sparrow Wallet app from the App Store and entered their seed phrases as instructed, only to have their Bitcoin transferred to scammer-controlled wallets.

The lawsuit points out that Sparrow Wallet is a desktop-only Bitcoin wallet available only on Windows, macOS, and Linux — it has never had an iOS version. Therefore, any app bearing its name on the App Store is by definition fraudulent. Yet Apple's review process failed to detect and remove these fake apps.

The complaint challenges Apple's long-standing marketing claim that the App Store is "a safe and trusted place to discover and download apps." The plaintiffs' lawyer stated: "This fraud succeeded precisely because Apple exploited the consumer trust it had deliberately cultivated through more than a decade of marketing." The filing also notes that Sparrow Wallet creator Craig Raw had publicly criticized Apple in early 2024 for allowing fake wallet apps to remain on the App Store.

The plaintiffs are seeking a jury trial, full reimbursement of their losses, punitive damages, and a court order requiring Apple to improve its app review processes and disclose App Store risks to users.

Apple responded by stating it takes swift action against apps impersonating others and confirmed that no Sparrow Wallet copycats currently exist on the App Store.

☢️ Antares Raises $470M to Build Nuclear Microreactors for US Military

Nuclear power startup Antares Nuclear announced Monday a $470 million Series C funding round to build small modular reactors (SMRs) for U.S. military bases. The round was co-led by Paradigm and Caffeinated Capital, with participation from Industrious Ventures, Point72 Ventures, and Shine Capital, consisting of $370 million in equity and $100 million in debt.

Antares is developing microreactors capable of producing between 100 kilowatts and 1 megawatt of electricity — enough to power up to 750 homes. The company is one of three finalists in the Pentagon's Advanced Nuclear Power for Installations (ANPI) program, which will test SMRs at Air Force bases in Colorado and Montana.

On June 4, Antares achieved a historic milestone when its Mark-0 test reactor reached criticality (self-sustaining nuclear fission) at Idaho National Laboratory — the first time a privately developed non-light-water reactor had achieved criticality in the United States in over four decades. Energy Secretary Chris Wright called it "the first time in more than four decades a new privately developed non-light-water reactor has reached criticality in the United States."

Antares plans to bring its first electricity-producing Mark-1 reactor online in 2027, with initial deployments at U.S. military installations targeted for 2028. The company has raised over $600 million to date, including a $96 million Series B and $30 million Series A.

The company's R1 microreactor is designed to operate for over six years without refueling, making it ideal for remote military installations and space applications. Antares has secured partnerships with the U.S. Air Force, Space Force, NASA, and the Department of Defense.

The new capital will accelerate the path from reactor demonstration to field deployment, directly supporting President Trump's Executive Order 14299, which requires an operational reactor at a domestic military installation by September 30, 2028.

⚡ Aug 10 Tech Update: TSMC in Talks to Buy Two AUO Plants, Firebird Opens CIS's Largest AI Factory, SK Hynix Responds to Chongqing Sale Report, Pony.ai Hits 100M Autonomous KM, Ant Hiring 80%+ AI Roles

On the afternoon of August 10, several major tech stories landed in quick succession: TSMC is reportedly in talks to acquire two AUO panel plants to expand advanced packaging capacity, with the deal potentially exceeding NT$30 billion; AI cloud platform Firebird announced the CIS region's largest AI factory is now operational in Armenia, with NVIDIA planning to invest; SK Hynix responded to Bloomberg's report on a possible Chongqing plant stake sale, saying there is "no final decision" yet; Pony.ai's global autonomous driving mileage surpassed 100 million km; and Ant Group's 2027 campus recruitment opened with more than 80% AI-related roles. Below are details of each story, with source publish times verified.

1. TSMC Reportedly in Talks to Buy Two AUO Plants to Expand Advanced Packaging

According to ITHome (published 15:15 Beijing time, Aug 10), market reports say TSMC will purchase AUO's Central Taiwan Science Park L7 fab (Gen 7.5) and L5C fab (Gen 5), which sit adjacent to its own Central Taiwan fabs, to expand advanced packaging capacity. The two companies are also expected to follow the "AU Optronics model" (群创模式) — where panel makers repurpose and upgrade older-generation plants, leveraging their existing large cleanrooms, high power allocations, and glass-substrate expertise to enter FOPLP (fan-out panel-level packaging) and AI advanced-packaging supply chains.

TSMC responded on Aug 9 that it "does not comment on market rumors," while AUO stressed it "does not comment on speculative reports." However, sources say the acquisition talks have entered the negotiation stage, with TSMC personnel already completing on-site inspections. Although no formal contract has been announced, the transaction is expected to exceed NT$30 billion (about RMB 6.288 billion at current rates), potentially setting a record for panel-plant sales in Taiwan. The two sides are expected to cooperate on FOPLP and the closely watched CoPoS, leveraging AUO's glass-substrate strengths to build an end-to-end advanced process and packaging base (source: ITHome).

2. CIS's Largest AI Factory Goes Live in Armenia: Firebird Lands NVIDIA Investment

According to ITHome (published 16:02 Beijing time, Aug 10), Firebird, an AI cloud platform focused on emerging-market countries, announced locally on the 8th that its largest AI factory in the entire CIS region, located in Hrazdan, Armenia, is now operational — just over six months after the project broke ground. The facility is built on NVIDIA AI GPUs using the NVIDIA DSX platform, supported by Dell servers, Schneider Electric power infrastructure, and Vertiv cooling systems, with initial customers including Perplexity AI.

Firebird plans to deploy 300MW of AI infrastructure capacity in Armenia by the end of 2027, implying more than 70,000 "Rubin" and "Blackwell" GPUs. It has also secured 125MW of capacity in Kazakhstan, with a global target of building 2GW of compute over roughly the next two years. The company previously received investment from Neocloud operator CoreWeave, and NVIDIA also plans to invest (source: ITHome).

3. SK Hynix Responds to Chongqing Plant Sale Report: No Final Decision

According to ITHome (published 15:43 Beijing time, Aug 10), SK Hynix issued a statement today responding to Bloomberg's report that it may sell a stake in its KRW 4 trillion Chongqing plant: "We are considering various measures to strengthen the competitiveness of our packaging business, but so far there has been no final decision. We will issue another announcement once specifics are confirmed, or within one month."

Bloomberg earlier reported that SK Hynix is evaluating its Chongqing plant at around US$3 billion, possibly selling a stake to bring in investors. The Chongqing facility is a key SK Hynix packaging and testing base in China, mainly handling DRAM and other memory chips. The response neither confirms nor denies a deal, emphasizing a focus on "strengthening packaging competitiveness," with further moves worth watching (source: ITHome).

4. Pony.ai's Global Autonomous Driving Mileage Surpasses 100 Million km

According to ITHome (published 15:30 Beijing time, Aug 10), Pony.ai announced today that its global autonomous driving mileage has surpassed 100 million km. Founded in 2016, the company listed on Nasdaq in November 2024 and on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in November 2025, operating Robotaxi and Robotruck services among others.

In its Q1 2026 results, total revenue was RMB 236 million, up 145.0% YoY and 17.6% QoQ; Robotaxi revenue reached RMB 59.12 million, up 395.4% YoY and 28.7% QoQ, with passenger-fare revenue up 456.5% YoY. In June, Pony.ai's seventh-generation Robotaxi debuted at the 2026 Chongqing International Auto Show. The 100-million-km milestone provides a strong safety and reliability case for scaled commercial Robotaxi operations (source: ITHome).

5. Ant Group Opens 2027 Campus Recruitment: Over 80% AI-Related Roles

According to ITHome (published 14:08 Beijing time, Aug 10), Ant Group announced today that it is opening its 2027 campus recruitment, targeting 2027 graduates from home and abroad (graduating between November 2026 and October 2027), with work locations covering Hangzhou, Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chongqing, Chengdu, Hong Kong, and more.

In this round, technical roles account for 80%, and over 80% are AI-related positions; graduates may use AI during written exams, and interviewers will also assess candidates' AI programming skills. Ant Group said graduates can upload resumes on its official site, where the system automatically breaks down skills and recommends matching roles. There is no limit on applications, with two preferences per submission, though the same department for a position cannot be re-applied within this season (source: ITHome).

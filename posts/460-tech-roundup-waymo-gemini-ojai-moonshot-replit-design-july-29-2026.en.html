⚡ July 29 Tech Roundup: Waymo Adds Gemini to Ojai Robotaxi, Moonshot AI Hits $35B, Replit Design Launches

🚕 Waymo Integrates Gemini AI Assistant into Ojai Robotaxi

Waymo announced today it is putting Google's Gemini AI assistant inside its Ojai purpose-built robotaxi for the first time, alongside the first major redesign of its rider interface in years. Passengers can summon the AI assistant by tapping a Gemini icon on the in-car screen to control cabin settings, access trip information, learn about Waymo technology, and ask general questions.

Key capabilities include:

  • Cabin Control: Adjust temperature, seat position, and ambient lighting via voice
  • Trip Information: Check ETA, route details, and nearby points of interest
  • Waymo Education: Learn about autonomous driving technology and safety records
  • General Q&A: Answer passenger questions on various topics

The assistant remains inactive until engaged, which Waymo positions as a privacy feature. It launches in beta with English-language support.

The Ojai is Waymo's ground-up designed vehicle built specifically for ride-hailing. Since first opening Ojai rides to the public in May, the Gemini integration and redesigned screen interface represent a major software-layer upgrade on top of the custom hardware. Waymo describes the new interface as a "lean-back" experience, with dynamic elements surfacing trip information, music controls, and cabin settings for a more relaxed ride.

App teardowns had previously revealed Waymo was testing Gemini integration late last year, complete with a leaked system prompt — now the feature has shipped to users.

💰 Moonshot AI Raises $3.5B at $35B Valuation, Plans $50B Pre-IPO Round

Chinese AI startup Moonshot AI has raised $3.5 billion in its latest funding round — far exceeding its initial $1-2 billion target — sending its valuation to $35 billion, according to Bloomberg. The oversubscribed round underscores surging investor interest in Chinese AI following the company's release of the Kimi K3 model.

Sources familiar with the matter say Moonshot AI is already planning a new funding round targeting a $50 billion pre-money valuation and aims to complete its Hong Kong IPO as early as this year. The Beijing-based startup's Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model, delivers performance approaching frontier models from Anthropic and OpenAI, making it the first open-weight model to reach frontier-level benchmarks.

The Kimi K3 release triggered a global tech stock selloff, with analysts calling it another "DeepSeek moment" — demonstrating that Chinese AI companies remain globally competitive despite US technology export bans and computing constraints. The model weights, supporting 1 million token context window, have been released for free download along with open-source infrastructure tools including MoonEP, FlashKDA, and AgentEnv.

However, the Trump administration has accused Moonshot AI of stealing intellectual property from Anthropic's Fable model to develop K3 and of acquiring restricted Nvidia AI chips through third-party channels. Moonshot AI denies these allegations.

🎨 Replit Launches Design Suite: From Idea to Design Through Natural Language

Cloud-based software creation platform Replit today unveiled Replit Design, a new creative suite that lets anyone turn ideas into visual designs at the speed of thought. At its core is "Ambient Intelligence," which guides and inspires users through every step of the design process without requiring traditional design skills.

Key features include:

  • Multi-Model Support: Choose from Claude, GPT-5, Gemini, Kimi, GLM and other leading AI models for design generation
  • Zero Handoff: Design, develop, and deploy entirely on Replit — no exports or rebuilds needed
  • Real-Time Preview: Natural language-driven live rendering with instant adjustments to layout, color, and logic
  • Available Now: Open to all Replit users starting today

To celebrate the launch, Replit is hosting a Designathon with over $50,000 in prizes for the most impressive designs created with the new tool. "A new creative class is rising: people who see an idea in their mind and refuse to wait to make it," said Replit CEO Amjad Masad. "Replit Design lets them create at the speed they think."

🧪 BrowserStack Launches Test Companion: Agentic AI Testing Inside the IDE

BrowserStack, the leading AI-native software testing platform, today announced Test Companion — agentic AI for test automation built directly into the IDE. Designed for QA teams and automation engineers, it accelerates test authoring, execution, debugging, and maintenance across web and mobile applications without requiring context switching.

Test Companion joins BrowserStack's growing AI portfolio, which includes BrowserStack AI (20+ agents across the testing lifecycle) and the open-source BrowserStack MCP Server (connecting the testing platform to GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and Claude). Test Companion extends agentic testing intelligence directly into the IDE workflow.

More than 1,000 teams already use Test Companion to author, debug, and maintain tests up to 4x faster. It is available now from the marketplace in VS Code, JetBrains, Cursor, and Antigravity, with zero setup required. "QA teams no longer need to switch between their IDE and testing platforms," said a BrowserStack spokesperson. "Test Companion makes testing a seamless part of the development workflow."
